From seed to pharmacy

One group, every step of the chain

From cultivation in Switzerland to the pharmacy counter and markets abroad, each step sits with a company of the Astrasana group.

  1. 01Cultivation

    Around 1,000 m² in Switzerland under a Swissmedic licence, following GACP.

  2. 02Production

    Processing and packaging, an in‑house quality control laboratory and GDP‑standard storage.

  3. 03Distribution

    Import and distribution of medical cannabis to pharmacies and clinics in Switzerland and the Czech Republic.

  4. 04Pharmacies

    THC flowers, standardised extracts and dronabinol for compounding, with expert advice.

    Products distributed in over 18 countries, with companies in the Czech Republic, Japan, the UK and Belgium.

Swiss cannabis cultivation

Astrasana Pharma AG cultivates high-quality medical cannabis on approximately 1,000 m² in Switzerland, operating under a Swissmedic licence. Production follows GACP, every batch is traceable from seed to harvest, and accredited laboratories test cannabinoids, terpenes and contaminants.

Production facility in Tagelswangen complete

Astrasana successfully completes the setup of its pharmaceutical production facility in Tagelswangen, Canton Zurich.

Climate-controlled cultivation rooms, pharmaceutical processing and packaging, an in-house quality control laboratory and GDP‑standard storage sit under one roof.

A vertically integrated Swiss pharmaceutical and cannabis company. From cultivation to pharmacy.
