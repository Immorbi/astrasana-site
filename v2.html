Vertically integrated, globally distributed

Astrasana Holding brings its companies together under one roof: cultivation and research, pharmaceutical production, distribution to pharmacies and clinics, and animal health.

As a pharmaceutical distribution specialist, Astrasana imports and distributes medical cannabis, supplying pharmacies and clinics in Switzerland and the Czech Republic. The company also cultivates cannabis for medical purposes in Switzerland.

As the first Swiss company, Astrasana maintains an exclusive partnership with the publicly listed GSI Creos corporation and distributes CBD products for both human and veterinary use.

With locations in Europe and Japan, Astrasana Holding remains competitive, agile and flexible. Our international network serves all market segments of the industry.

Swiss cannabis cultivation

Astrasana Pharma AG cultivates high-quality medical cannabis on approximately 1,000 m² in Switzerland, operating under a Swissmedic licence. Production follows GACP, every batch is traceable from seed to harvest, and accredited laboratories test cannabinoids, terpenes and contaminants.

Production facility in Tagelswangen complete

Astrasana successfully completes the setup of its pharmaceutical production facility in Tagelswangen, Canton Zurich.

Climate-controlled cultivation rooms, pharmaceutical processing and packaging, an in-house quality control laboratory and GDP‑standard storage sit under one roof.

How patients in Switzerland access their medical cannabis

Since August 2022, physicians in Switzerland can prescribe medical cannabis directly. No special federal permit is required.

  1. 01Doctor consultation

    The treating physician evaluates whether medical cannabis is an appropriate treatment option based on the patient's condition and history.

  2. 02Prescription

    The physician issues a narcotics prescription (Betäubungsmittelrezept) specifying the product, dosage, and treatment plan.

  3. 03Pharmacy dispensing

    The patient fills their prescription at a licensed pharmacy that carries pharmaceutical-grade medical cannabis products.

  4. 04Ongoing treatment

    Regular follow-ups with the prescribing physician ensure optimal dosing, monitor efficacy, and adjust the treatment plan as needed.
