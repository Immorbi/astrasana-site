Our group

Companies

Vertically integrated, globally distributed

Astrasana Holding brings its companies together under one roof: cultivation and research, pharmaceutical production, distribution to pharmacies and clinics, and animal health.

As a pharmaceutical distribution specialist, Astrasana imports and distributes medical cannabis, supplying pharmacies and clinics in Switzerland and the Czech Republic. The company also cultivates cannabis for medical purposes in Switzerland.

As the first Swiss company, Astrasana maintains an exclusive partnership with the publicly listed GSI Creos corporation and distributes CBD products for both human and veterinary use.

With locations in Europe and Japan, Astrasana Holding remains competitive, agile and flexible. Our international network serves all market segments of the industry.
